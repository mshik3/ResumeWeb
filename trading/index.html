A trading bot that runs itself

Five strategies from papers, one allocator deciding who gets the money, and a lot of bugs I didn't know trading could have. Paper money for now.

Paper account on Alpaca, started at $100,000. Eight months is not a track record and none of this is advice.

The strategies

StrategyIdeaWeight now
Keller DAATwo "canary" assets (emerging markets and bonds) decide how defensive to be. Keller & Keuning, 2018.30%
Faber GTAAHold each asset class only while it's above its 10-month average. Faber, 2006.29%
Crypto trendHold BTC and ETH above their 20-day average, sized by volatility. Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen, 2012.19%
Dual MomentumHold the strongest US equity ETF on 12-month momentum, or short-term Treasuries if nothing beats cash. Antonacci.12%
Connors RSI-2Buy sharp dips in ETFs that are still in an uptrend, sell the bounce. Connors & Alvarez, 2008.10%

They were picked to disagree with each other: monthly trend, daily mean reversion, and crypto barely correlate. A meta-allocator sits on top. Once a strategy has 60 days of history, money shifts toward the ones with better rolling Sharpe, and any sleeve that drifts more than 20% off its target gets trimmed back.

Guardrails

  • Checks it's pointed at the paper account before every single trade.
  • Down 10%: no new trades. Down 15%: cancel everything and go to cash.
  • Tracks tax lots and skips any buy that would trigger a wash sale. Sells long-term lots first.
  • Reconciles its own books against the broker every run and parks anything it can't explain.
  • Posts every run to a Discord channel, so I find out about problems before the P&L does.

How it's built

A Cloudflare Worker on a weekday cron at 9:45 ET, with D1 holding positions, tax lots, orders, signals, and daily snapshots. It's built spec-first: write a spec, turn it into a plan, implement against tests. The repo has 197 tests and production invariant checks that fail loudly when the books don't add up.

Everything that broke

The commit log is the honest part.

  • Mar 13First version. Tests for the first nine bugs, including two strategies fighting over the same position.
  • Mar 20Gave each strategy its own cash so they stop spending each other's money. Mark-to-market P&L per strategy.
  • May 3The bot wrote BTC/USD, the broker said BTCUSD. Same coin, two spellings, and positions quietly went missing. Symbols now get normalized on the way into the database.
  • May 18Daily reconciliation against the broker, and positions update from actual fills instead of from what I asked for.
  • Jul 8Strategies were bleeding into each other's positions, drawdown was feeding back into itself, and signals were on the wrong timezone.
  • Aug 20Crypto cost basis was valued wrong. Added invariant checks so the next accounting bug can't hide.
  • Sep 3Friday runs had never fired. Cloudflare numbers weekdays starting with Sunday as 1, so my "1-5" ran Sunday to Thursday for six months. Also: drawdown is now measured against real capital.
  • Sep 28The crypto strategy couldn't see its own positions, so it bought again every day and never sold. Also found a test mock that was swallowing failed sells.
